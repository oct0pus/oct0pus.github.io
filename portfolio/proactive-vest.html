Overview
ProActive is a reusable, self-inflating vest for people with autism and sensory sensitivities. I designed the app that pairs with it. I turned the research team's findings into features, then designed the flows, the screens and the colour system, so wearers and caregivers can see what the vest senses, control it, and learn from each episode all while following Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).
How the vest works
The ProActive prototype
Sensory overload happens in everyday places.

I.Problem and Goalsp. 1−
I turned the startup's research into the app's brief. Because the app and the vest are being built at the same time, I keep reiterating the design as the hardware changes and the research team learns more. With this user base, I made accessibility my first priority throughout.
Goals
01A visible, accessible emergency help screen
02A calming colour scheme that still passes contrast checks
03Many functions, with navigation that stays clear
Fig. 1 · Severe episode: the two actions that matter come first.
Fig. 2 · Resolved: a plain summary of what happened.
Two buttons, nothing else
I cut this screen down to two actions. In the moment, the wearer only needs to stop the vest or start calming down; everything else waits until the episode is over.
II. Research p. 2 −
The research team ran user interviews and A/B tests. My role was to turn what they found into design decisions.
What they found → what I designed
01Wearers and caregivers couldn't tell what caused their stress → Trigger analysis that names the likely cause of each episode
02Wearers needed guidance when stress peaked → Guided meditation offered at the moment stress rises
03Wearers wanted privacy from caregivers, as some episodes were personal → Caregiver notes kept alongside a private journal
Fig. 3 · Episode detail: what happened, likely triggers and suggestions.
Fig. 4 · Triggers: patterns across weeks, with tips that may help.
From data to next steps
I made every insight end in something the wearer can do, such as adjusting the vest's sensitivity, with a quick “Helpful?” check.

V. Reflection p. 5 −
This was my first time working with a startup on an ongoing basis. As a freelancer I usually run the research myself; here I took input from the research team. The team's capabilities kept changing, so I recreated the app many times to match new goals. Throughout this process I learned more about designing accessible screens, especially following the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).
